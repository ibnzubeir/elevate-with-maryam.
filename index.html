WELCOME TO ELEVATE

Every woman deserves to find herself again.

Women spend so much of their lives caring for everyone around them. Their families. Their children. Their responsibilities. Their communities.

But somewhere along the way, many women forget to care for the woman in the mirror.

Elevate with Maryam is about remembering her.

HER HERO STORY

From losing herself to finding her voice.

This is Maryam's journey.

THE BEGINNING

The girl who believed she could do anything.

Assalamu Alaikum everyone. ❤️

I was a strong, ambitious and confident little girl. I believed that whatever I wanted to achieve, I could achieve.

Life, however, taught me lessons I never expected.

LIFE CHANGED

Wife. Mother. Caregiver.

I married very young and learned patience, responsibility and sacrifice.

I became a mother of five — two girls and three boys.

I loved my family deeply, but somewhere along the way, I slowly lost myself.

THE TURNING POINT

I couldn't recognize the woman I had become.

I became dependent on the approval of others, especially my husband.

The strong, ambitious girl I once was seemed to slowly disappear.

MY WELLNESS JOURNEY

Then came another chapter.

I experienced challenges with my wellbeing and began searching for ways to feel better and regain my confidence.

I tried different approaches, but I was still searching for something that would help me take another step forward.

THE MOMENT

"Papi, 25K? That's too much!"

My father was already using the program. He encouraged me to try it.

Instead of buying it myself, he gifted it to me.

And that became one of the moments that changed my direction.

REDISCOVERY

I started finding myself again.

As I continued my journey, I began noticing changes in how I felt, my energy and my confidence.

But the biggest transformation wasn't something I could measure.

I started finding Maryam again.
