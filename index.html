A MESSAGE FOR WOMEN

You spend so much time caring for everyone else.

But somewhere along the way, you may have forgotten to care for yourself.

Elevate with Maryam is a space created to remind women that their dreams, confidence, health, growth and happiness matter too.

It is about rediscovering yourself, building confidence, creating purpose and helping other women rise along the way.

HER STORY

The girl who found herself again.

This is Maryam's journey — a story of motherhood, challenges, transformation, courage and purpose.

01

The ambitious little girl

Maryam describes herself as a strong, ambitious and confident little girl who believed she could achieve whatever she put her mind to.

02

Marriage & motherhood

She married young and learned patience, responsibility and sacrifice. She eventually became a mother of five — two girls and three boys.

03

Losing herself

Somewhere along the way, Maryam felt that she had lost the strong and ambitious girl she once was. She became increasingly dependent on the approval of others.

04
