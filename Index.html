HER HERO STORY

I lost myself. Then I found myself again.

MARYAM ZUBEIR Founder · Elevate with Maryam

Assalamu Alaikum everyone. ❤️

I was a strong, ambitious and confident little girl. I believed that if I wanted something, I could achieve it.

Then life happened.

I got married very young, and I learned patience, responsibility and sacrifice. I became a mother of five — two girls and three boys.

Somewhere along the way, I lost myself.

I became dependent on the approval of others, especially my husband. The strong, ambitious girl I once knew slowly started disappearing.

“The woman I used to be was still inside me. I just needed to find her again.”

Then came another chapter in my life.

I was struggling with my health, my energy, my confidence and my menstrual health. I tried different approaches, including pills and herbal remedies, hoping something would change.

My father was already using the program. One day he looked at me and said:

“Maryam, why don't you try it?” — Dad

And I said: “Papi, 25K? That's too much!”

But he believed in me enough to gift it to me.

That moment became a turning point.

As I continued my journey, I began noticing changes in my energy, confidence and overall wellbeing.

But the biggest transformation wasn't something I could see in a mirror.

I started finding myself again.

The ambitious girl was coming back.

My father then encouraged me to build the business. I was scared. I doubted myself.

But he believed in me.

So I decided to take a step forward.
